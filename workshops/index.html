Practical negotiation workshops in Zurich

Negotiation is a skill that can be developed through preparation, practice and reflection. Whether you are discussing a salary, working on a business deal, managing a disagreement or collaborating in a team, the ability to negotiate effectively can make a meaningful difference.

Negotiation Club Zurich creates opportunities for students, young professionals and anyone interested in negotiation to practise in a structured and supportive environment.

Our approach combines short inputs on established negotiation concepts with realistic exercises, role-play simulations and constructive peer feedback.

Why practise negotiation?

Understanding negotiation theory is valuable. Being able to apply it in a real conversation is what makes the learning practical.

Build confidence

Practise expressing your interests, making proposals and responding to different negotiation situations.

Improve communication

Develop active listening, effective questioning and the ability to understand different perspectives.

Think strategically

Learn to prepare, evaluate alternatives and identify opportunities to create value for everyone involved.

Negotiation workshop topics

Our practical learning activities can explore different negotiation concepts and techniques. The specific topics depend on the session and its learning objectives.

Negotiation preparation

Define your objectives, understand your priorities, identify the other party's interests and develop a clear negotiation strategy.

BATNA and ZOPA

Understand your Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement (BATNA) and explore the Zone of Possible Agreement (ZOPA).

Anchoring and first offers

Explore how initial offers influence negotiations, when to make the first offer and how to respond to an anchor.

Interests and value creation

Move beyond fixed positions, uncover underlying interests and identify opportunities for mutually beneficial outcomes.

Communication and persuasion

Practise active listening, asking effective questions, presenting proposals and communicating persuasively.

Closing agreements

Evaluate possible solutions, manage concessions and work towards clear and sustainable agreements.

Learn through negotiation simulations

A central part of practical negotiation learning is the opportunity to experience a negotiation from different perspectives. Role-play simulations allow participants to test approaches, make decisions and reflect on the results.

Salary negotiations

Explore how to prepare for a salary conversation, communicate your value and discuss expectations.

Business negotiations

Practise commercial discussions involving pricing, competing interests, proposals and potential agreements.

Team negotiations

Work through situations involving different priorities, limited resources and the need to find common ground.

Everyday negotiations

Explore practical conversations involving collaboration, expectations, disagreements and personal interests.

How a negotiation workshop works

Our session structure is designed to move from understanding a concept to applying it in practice.

  • 1. Introduction A short introduction to the negotiation topic and the key concepts.
  • 2. Preparation Participants review a negotiation case, identify their interests and prepare their approach.
  • 3. Negotiation Participants negotiate in pairs or small groups using assigned roles and objectives.
  • 4. Debrief Discuss the negotiation process, decisions, outcomes and alternative strategies.
  • 5. Reflection Identify lessons and techniques that can be applied to future conversations.

Frequently asked questions

What happens in a negotiation workshop?

A workshop can combine short theoretical inputs, practical exercises, role-play simulations and structured reflection. Participants have the opportunity to practise negotiation techniques and learn from different perspectives.

Do I need previous negotiation experience?

No. Introductory activities can be suitable for beginners. More focused exercises can explore specific negotiation frameworks and techniques.

Which negotiation skills can I practise?

Topics can include preparation, BATNA, ZOPA, anchoring, active listening, asking questions, creating value and closing agreements.

Who can participate in the workshops?

Negotiation Club Zurich welcomes students, young professionals and people interested in developing practical negotiation skills. We also welcome enquiries from organisations interested in possible collaborations.
