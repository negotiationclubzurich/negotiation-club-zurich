About the club

Negotiation Club Zurich brings together people who want to practice negotiation in a safe, structured and friendly setting. Whether you are preparing for a salary talk, a business deal or a difficult conversation, you will find a place to try, fail and improve.

Our sessions combine short inputs on proven frameworks with live role-plays and honest peer feedback. Every member leaves with tools they can use the next day.

What we do

Role-play simulations

Negotiate realistic cases in pairs and teams, then debrief what worked.

Skills workshops

Learn preparation, anchoring, BATNA, creating value and closing the deal.

Networking

Meet motivated people from different universities, companies and industries.

How a session works

  • Warm-upShort introduction to the topic and a key concept
  • NegotiateLive case in small groups with assigned roles
  • DebriefFeedback, lessons learned and open discussion
  • ConnectInformal exchange after the session
