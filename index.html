About the club

Negotiation Club Zurich brings together people who want to practise negotiation in a safe, structured and friendly setting. Whether you are preparing for a salary talk, a business deal or a difficult conversation, you will find a place to try, fail and improve.

Our sessions combine short inputs on proven frameworks with live role-plays and honest peer feedback. Every member leaves with tools they can use the next day.

Where
Zurich, Switzerland
Language
English
Who can join
Students, young professionals and anyone curious about negotiation
Format
In-person sessions and workshops

What we do

Role-play simulations

Negotiate realistic cases in pairs and teams, then debrief what worked and what could be improved.

Skills workshops

Learn practical approaches to preparation, anchoring, BATNA, creating value and closing an agreement.

Networking

Meet motivated people from different universities, companies and industries who share an interest in negotiation.

How a session works

  • Warm-upShort introduction to the topic and a key concept
  • NegotiateLive case in small groups with assigned roles
  • DebriefFeedback, lessons learned and open discussion
  • ConnectInformal exchange after the session

Frequently asked questions

What is Negotiation Club Zurich?

Negotiation Club Zurich is an English-speaking community in Zurich where students and professionals can develop practical negotiation skills through interactive sessions, role-play simulations and peer feedback.

Who can participate?

Students, young professionals and anyone interested in improving their negotiation skills are welcome. You do not need previous negotiation experience.

Are the sessions held in English?

Yes. English is the working language of the club.

Can a university or company collaborate with the club?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from universities, student organisations, companies and other organisations interested in negotiation workshops, joint events or other forms of collaboration.
