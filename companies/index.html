Negotiation is a business skill

Negotiation is part of everyday business. Teams negotiate with clients, suppliers, colleagues and business partners. They navigate competing interests, manage expectations and work towards agreements.

Strong negotiation requires more than persuasive communication. It involves preparation, listening, understanding interests, evaluating alternatives and identifying opportunities to create value.

Negotiation Club Zurich provides a setting to practise these capabilities through structured exercises, role-play simulations and peer learning.

Negotiation topics

Depending on the objectives of a potential workshop, exercises can cover a range of practical negotiation frameworks.

  • Preparation and objectives Define priorities, interests and negotiation strategies.
  • BATNA and alternatives Understand alternatives and assess possible outcomes.
  • Anchoring and offers Develop approaches to making and responding to proposals.
  • Value creation Identify shared interests and opportunities for mutual gains.
  • Managing difficult conversations Navigate disagreement and competing expectations constructively.
  • Closing agreements Evaluate options and work towards clear, workable agreements.

Learning through practice

Negotiation skills are developed by applying concepts, reflecting on decisions and practising different approaches. Our session format is built around active participation.

  • 1. Introduce Explore a negotiation concept or framework.
  • 2. Prepare Review a case, define objectives and plan an approach.
  • 3. Practise Negotiate in pairs or small groups using assigned roles.
  • 4. Reflect Discuss decisions, outcomes and alternative strategies.
  • 5. Apply Identify lessons that can be transferred to professional situations.
